When you cannot name what is wrong, the only move left is "try again" — and that either misses the point or rewrites what was already right.

You paste an unsatisfying answer and the reasoning behind it — it names what went wrong and how to check, then hands you a retake note (an optimization & checking instruction) you copy in full and paste back into the original chat, so the original AI answers better.

It does not write the answer for you. Your habits, your context and the result you want all stay in the original AI's chat.

Five modes

ReshootFirst round, or the subject does not hold — full rewrite
Pick-upLater rounds — change only what you name, keep the rest
Final cutDeadlock or conflicting versions — make the trade-off call
Color gradingSubject holds — polish expression and style only
PackagingMeets the bar — turn it into a summary, map or chart

You never have to name a mode — it reads the context and picks the step.

What you get

One retake note — copy it in full and paste it at the end of the original chat.

Illustration only

A retake note carries two more sections at the end:

  • Domain-specific criteria — set the bar before rewriting. The target AI writes the acceptance criteria for your field first, then works to them, so the output does not merely satisfy the literal wording.
  • Pre-delivery self-check — check items that can be judged pass/fail. It runs them before handing anything over.
